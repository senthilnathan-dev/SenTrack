0.5.0 | 2026-10-20 | First internal-testing build

Added

  • Repeating monthly entries in Finance (salary, rent, SIPs, EMIs). Due ones appear as "Due now" on the Dashboard and are added or skipped with a tap. A reminder can ring on the day.
  • User guide inside the app (Settings, User guide).
  • "What's new" notes shown once after each update.
  • A proper app icon, including the themed (monochrome) version.

Changed

  • Privacy policy and terms rewritten to cover every feature (Work location, Career, AI, model download, clipboard).
  • Privacy policy, terms, user guide and release notes are now plain text files shared by the app and the published web pages, so they cannot drift apart.

Fixed

  • Reminders and automatic backups that repeat monthly can now use the 29th, 30th or 31st. In shorter months they run on that month's last day (instead of being limited to the 28th).
  • Repeating entries can now fall on any day of the month, including the 29th, 30th, 31st and "Last day of the month". In shorter months they use that month's last day, and the reminder follows the same rule.

Security

  • Workspace links accept only web addresses (http and https).
  • Text copied from the AI assistant is marked as sensitive on the clipboard.
  • Release builds keep the classes the database and the on-device AI runtime need.
  • Automatic tests now check that old databases upgrade to the current one without losing data.

0.4.0 | 2026-10-20 | Development build

Added

  • Career: learning, certificates with renewal reminders, job hunt with interview reminders, resume versions, a workspace of apps and sites you use, and joining and leaving checklists.
  • Work: companies, punch in and out with one-tap location, leave policy and calendar, tasks with a daily summary, appreciation records with proof files, and permissions.
  • Personal: plans, wishlist and bucket list, achievements, important dates with reminders, a timeline, habits with streaks, and a modern notes editor.
  • AI assistant, off by default: an on-device model set up with one tap and tested on your phone, or a cloud provider with your own key. Sparkle buttons in notes, plans, work, career and finance. Fast, Normal and Accurate answer styles.
  • Settings, About SenTrack: version, privacy policy, terms, open-source licences.
  • Reminders that ring even while the app is locked.
